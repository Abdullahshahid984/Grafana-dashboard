1. Install all Windows updates
Open Settings > Windows Update and click "Check for updates." Let it download and install everything, then click "Restart now" when it asks. After the restart, go back to Windows Update and check again. Keep repeating this until it says "You're up to date." This usually takes 2 to 4 rounds.
Also open Windows Update > Advanced options > Optional updates and install any driver or firmware updates listed there, then restart.

2. Sync the device with company policies
Open Settings > Accounts > Access work or school. Click your connected work account (it will say something like "Connected to [Organization] Azure AD"), then click "Info." Scroll down to "Device sync status" and click "Sync." Wait for it to show the latest sync time.
You can also sync from the Company Portal app: open Company Portal, go to Settings, and click "Sync."

3. Wait, restart, and sync again
Policies, apps, and security settings can take 15 to 30 minutes to arrive. After waiting, restart the laptop and run the sync from step 2 one more time.
