Prod backup frequency: Azure allows at most 500 snapshots per Azure Disk volume at a time: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/backup/azure-kubernetes-service-backup-troubleshoot#usererrorpvsnapshotlimitreached
4-hourly backups with 90-day retention would keep 540, so that is not possible for prod. Two options that fit:
- Every 6 hours with 90-day retention = 360 snapshots (4 restore points a day)
- Every 8 hours with 90-day retention = 270 snapshots (3 restore points a day)
I added a check in backup.tf that fails the plan if a policy would keep more than 480. Dev and non-production stay at 4-hourly with 7-day retention. Which one would you prefer for prod, 6-hourly or 8-hourly?
