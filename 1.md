Prod backup frequency: Azure allows at most 500 snapshots per Azure Disk volume at a time ([Microsoft: AKS backup troubleshooting, UserErrorPVSnapshotLimitReached]([url](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/backup/azure-kubernetes-service-backup-troubleshoot#usererrorpvsnapshotlimitreached))). 4-hourly backups with 90-day retention would keep 540, so for prod I’m proposing every 8 hours with 90-day retention (270 snapshots). I added a check in backup.tf that fails the plan if a policy would keep more than 480. Dev stays at 4-hourly with 7-day retention. Does anyone disagree with 8-hourly for prod?
