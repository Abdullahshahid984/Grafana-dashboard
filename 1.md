```
ApiManagementGatewayLogs
| where TimeGenerated between (datetime(2026-11-06T15:30:00Z) .. datetime(2026-11-06T18:30:00Z))
| summarize requests=count(),
            err_5xx=countif(ResponseCode >= 500),
            p95_backend_ms=percentile(BackendTime, 95),
            p95_apim_ms=percentile(TotalTime - BackendTime, 95)
            by bin(TimeGenerated, 5m)
| order by TimeGenerated asc
```
